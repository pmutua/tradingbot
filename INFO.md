## Example Strategy Using RSI

Here's a simple example of how you might use the RSI indicator for trading decisions:

- Buy Signal: If RSI drops below 30 and then crosses back above 30, it could indicate an oversold condition and a potential buying opportunity.
- Sell Signal: If RSI rises above 70 and then crosses back below 70, it could indicate an overbought condition and a potential selling opportunity.
